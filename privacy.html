3. The Heistly Clips app

The Heistly Clips app (the "App") is a private tool. It connects only accounts that we own or manage, and it is not offered to other users.

What the App receives from the platforms:

  • basic account information of our own accounts, such as account ID, username and display name;
  • access tokens that allow the App to upload videos to these accounts;
  • IDs, links and statistics (for example views, likes and viewer countries) of posts made by these accounts.

What we use it for: uploading our own videos to our own accounts and evaluating how our own posts perform. We do not use the data for advertising, we do not sell it, and we do not share it with anyone except the platform concerned when we upload a video.

Where it is stored: only on our own computer. Access tokens are stored in a file that only our user account can read.

How long: access tokens until they expire or access is revoked; post statistics for as long as we need them to evaluate our own posts. If access is revoked, we delete data obtained through that platform's API within 30 days.

4. Platforms used by the App

The platforms process data under their own privacy policies:

  • TikTok: TikTok Privacy Policy. Access can be revoked in the TikTok app settings.
  • Instagram (Meta): Instagram Privacy Policy. Access can be revoked in the Instagram and Meta Business settings.
  • YouTube (Google): the App uses YouTube API Services. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy, and the YouTube Terms of Service apply. In addition to our own deletion procedure, access by the App can be revoked at any time on the Google security settings page.

The legal basis for processing in the App is our legitimate interest in publishing and evaluating our own content (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).
