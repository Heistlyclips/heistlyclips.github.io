About the clips

Every clip names the streamer it comes from. Heistly Clips is a fan project and is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by Rockstar Games, Take-Two Interactive, Twitch or the streamers shown.

Removal requests

If you are a streamer or rights holder and want a clip removed, email heistlyclips@gmail.com with the link to the post. We remove it promptly.

Our app

We use a small private tool, the Heistly Clips app, to upload our own videos to our own accounts through the official TikTok, Instagram and YouTube APIs and to read the statistics of our own posts. It is not offered to anyone else. See the Terms of Service and the Privacy Policy.
